Best Parks in Fulshear, TX

Fulshear, Texas offers beautiful parks and outdoor spaces, especially within its master-planned communities. Here's your complete guide.

Plan a Visit to a City-Managed Park

The City of Fulshear's Parks & Recreation page is the best starting point for current facilities, field and community-center rentals, and department contact details. The City's current list includes Frances Smart Park, Irene Stern Community Center, Eagle Landing Park, and Primrose Park.

  • Eagle Landing Park: check the official page for soccer-field, pavilion, concessions, availability, and reservation information.
  • Primrose Park: check the official page for baseball/softball field availability, open-play guidance, and rental policies.
  • Irene Stern Community Center and Park: the City posts separate information for community-center and pavilion rentals.
  • Parks and Pathways planning documents: use these City documents to review longer-term park and pathway planning.

Before you go: hours, field access, programming, and rental availability can change, so check the City pages before leaving. Master-planned community amenities may have separate resident or guest access rules. Source checked August 25, 2026.

Master- Planned Community Parks

Cross Creek Ranch Parks

Multiple parks throughout the community with playgrounds, trails, and picnic areas. The central park features a lazy river and resort-style pool.

Fulbrook Parks

Beautiful green spaces, walking trails, and fishing lakes. Community center with fitness facilities.

Westin Lakes Parks

Parks with playgrounds, sports fields, and scenic lake views.

Public Parks

  • Fulshear Park - Main public park with sports fields
  • Timber Lane Park - Neighborhood park with trails

Things to Do

Parks in Fulshear offer hiking trails, fishing, playgrounds, sports courts, and community events throughout the year.
